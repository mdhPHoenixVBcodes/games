# Ursina 3D Adventure Game - Player Guide

## Overview
Welcome to the 3D Adventure Game built with the Ursina Engine! This game is a multi-level action-survival experience where you must fight enemies, dodge cannonballs, and interact with NPCs to progress through different dimensions.

## Controls
* **W, A, S, D:** Move around
* **Spacebar:** Jump
* **Mouse Movement:** Look around (Camera is locked to the center)
* **Left Mouse Click:** Attack with Sword
* **Right Mouse Click:** Shoot Arrow with Bow *(Unlocked in Level 2)*
* **F:** Interact with NPCs
* **8:** Save Game
* **9:** Load Game
* **0:** Pause / Unpause
* **/**: Reset Game / Return to Spawn
* **Escape:** Quit Game

## Game Progression & Levels

### Phase 1: The Green Plains (Level 1)
* **Objective:** Survive and defeat 5 Red Cube enemies.
* **Details:** Enemies will continually spawn around you. Use your sword (Left Click) to fend them off. Once you kill 5 enemies, a **Magenta Portal** will open nearby. Step into the portal to proceed.

### Phase 2: The Safe Zone (Level 2)
* **Objective:** Acquire a ranged weapon and unlock the next area.
* **Details:** * Find the **Chef** (Orange Sphere) and press **F** to interact. He will give you his Bow, allowing you to use Right Click to shoot arrows.
    * After getting the bow, find the **Manager** (Green Sphere) and interact with him (**F**). He will open the **Cyan Portal** to the next dimension.

### Phase 3: The Cannon Hallway (Level 3)
* **Objective:** Destroy the 3 Cannons blocking your path.
* **Details:** You will spawn in a long, walled hallway. At the end, three Red Cannons are firing Orange Spheres at you. 
    * **Hint:** The spheres deal heavy damage! Shoot the spheres out of the air using your arrows, or shoot the cannons to destroy them. Once all 3 cannons are destroyed, the massive Orange Door at the end of the hallway will open.

### Phase 4: The Final Arena (Level 4)
* **Objective:** Clear the dungeon!
* **Details:** Enter the arena past the orange door. You will be ambushed by both Red Cube enemies and new Cannons. Survive the onslaught and destroy the final Cannons to achieve victory!

## Mechanics & Tips
* **Health:** You have 100 HP. If it drops to 0, you will die. If you have a save file, the game will automatically load it. Otherwise, it will reset your progress.
* **Saving:** Press **8** at any time to save your exact position, health, inventory (bow), and level progress.
* **Combat:** Your sword hits anything within a short radius in front of you. Arrows travel fast and are perfect for taking out cannons from a safe distance.

Good luck, and have fun!
